Students

Grades 6–12

Any experience level. Come for a workshop, stay for a project, or just see what the club is like before deciding.

  • No coding or AI background needed
  • Events are free
  • Bring a friend
Mentors

College students

Help younger students work through a first dataset or a first robot. A few hours around an event goes a long way.

  • Data science, CS, health, or engineering backgrounds
  • Support at workshops and challenges
  • Work alongside UCLA faculty mentors

What happens next

  1. Fill in the form. A few minutes, and it tells us what you're curious about.
  2. We email you. You'll hear about the next workshop or panel that fits your interests.
  3. Show up. Come to an event, meet people, and decide from there.
