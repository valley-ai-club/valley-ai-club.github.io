Built for the Valley

A local place to explore AI—without the hype.

We’re creating a youth-led club where students can understand how AI works, build useful projects, and think carefully about how technology can support people and communities across the San Fernando Valley.

Middle school and high school students are welcome. You do not need prior coding or AI experience—just an interest in learning with others.

LearnStart with clear ideas

Build confidence through approachable lessons and examples.

BuildTurn questions into projects

Use coding, data, simulation, and robotics to test what is possible.

ServeStay connected to place

Choose projects that matter to students, families, and the Valley.

What we’ll explore

AI is bigger than one kind of project.

Our themes connect technical skills with real questions. Students can try one area or move between all three.

01

  • Health data
  • Prediction
  • Responsible AI
02

Coding, Simulation & Robotics

Learn programming by modeling real systems, testing ideas, and connecting code to the physical world.

  • Programming
  • Simulation
  • Robotics
