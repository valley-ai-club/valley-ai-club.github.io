Who it's for

Everyone starts somewhere.

Middle school and high school students are welcome. You do not need prior coding or AI experience—just an interest in learning with others.

College students who want to mentor are welcome too. Much of what the club does works best when someone a few years ahead is sitting alongside a student working through their first project.

LearnStart with clear ideas

Build confidence through approachable lessons and examples.

BuildTurn questions into projects

Use coding, data, simulation, and robotics to test what is possible.

ServeStay connected to place

Choose projects that matter to students, families, and the Valley.

Mentorship

Student-led, with experienced guidance.

The club is led by high school students and guided by UCLA faculty and student mentors with experience in Data Science and AI for Health education. We work in collaboration with CHEER.

The Valley AI Club is a community initiative and does not represent an official UCLA program.
