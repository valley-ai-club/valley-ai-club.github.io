Grades 6–12 Beginners welcome 6 monthly meetings 2 hours each

Each monthly meeting

Learn together. Then try it yourself.

Students with no coding experience and students who already know some Python are welcome. Every meeting includes a beginner task, an optional challenge, and a working starting point so you can rejoin after an absence.

  1. First 60 minutesTeaching & live demos

    Learn one main skill and see it in action.

  2. Next 60 minutesHands-on office hours

    Work on the monthly exercise at your own pace, with help from mentors.

What you’ll build: a project website, a Python analysis notebook, two related charts, and a simple prediction model. Keep a short log of how you used AI and checked its suggestions.

Our toolkit

Use AI. Check its work. Build your own model.

ChatGPT is our main assistant for drafting, coding, debugging, and checking explanations. GitHub and GitHub Pages save and share our work. Google Colab runs Python notebooks, pandas and matplotlib help us analyze and plot data, and scikit-learn trains the first predictive model.

GitHub Copilot is an optional coding assistant for experienced students. Paid AI plans are not required. Students use eligible, approved accounts; instructor demonstrations, prepared responses, and local files provide alternatives when individual access is unavailable.

Real data, careful conclusions.

We use a prepared teaching extract and aggregate counts from Los Angeles 311 open data. Recorded requests do not capture every community need, and a closed ticket does not necessarily mean completed service. Our public charts use aggregate data.

The curriculum adapts practical topics from UCLA Biostatistics 203B.
